      Verdict + Scores

      The system is trained on a labeled dataset of real and fake news articles. Text is cleaned, vectorized with TF-IDF, and passed through the best classifier (Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, Random Forest, SVM, Voting Ensemble). URLs are scraped and domain-checked. Results, clickbait and readability scores are stored in SQLite. Your 👍/👎 feedback tracks real-world accuracy.

      About Us & Digital Literacy
      How to spot fake news — a checklist before you hit "share".
      01

      Check the Source

      Is it a known, credible publication? Unfamiliar domains mimicking real news sites are a red flag.

      02

      Read Past the Headline

      Sensational headlines are designed for clicks, not accuracy. Read the full article before reacting.

      03

      Check the Date

      Old stories are often recirculated out of context to mislead during current events.

      04

      Verify the Author

      Search the author's name — legitimate journalists have a traceable publishing history.

      05

      Cross-Check Elsewhere

      If only one obscure source is reporting a big claim, treat it with suspicion.

      06

      Watch for Emotional Language

      Excessive caps, exclamation marks, and fear-based wording are common tactics — exactly what our clickbait meter measures.
